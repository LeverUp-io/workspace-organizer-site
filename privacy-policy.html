Workspace Organizer Privacy Policy

This policy explains what the Workspace Organizer Chrome extension stores, why, who can see it, and how to have it deleted.

We storeYour Google name, email address and profile picture, and the workspaces you create: their names, the addresses and titles of their tabs, resources, notes and tasks.
We don'tRead your browsing history outside your workspaces, access your Gmail or Google Drive, use analytics or ads, or sell any data.
Who sees itOnly you, and the people you share a workspace with.
WhereA database hosted by Supabase in Frankfurt, Germany (EU).

1. Information the extension collects

Your account. When you sign in with Google, the extension receives your name, email address and profile picture. It uses them to create your account, show who you are, and let others invite you to a workspace. It requests no other Google permissions.

Your workspaces. For each workspace you create or join, the extension stores:

  • the workspace's name and its members;
  • the address, title and icon of the tabs open in that workspace, saved automatically so they can be restored when you switch back or use another computer;
  • the resources (links), notes and tasks you add.

On your device. The extension also keeps a local copy of your workspace tabs, your dashboard layout and your settings in Chrome's extension storage on your computer. This data doesn't leave your device except as described above.

Tabs only become part of a workspace while that workspace is active. The extension doesn't record your browsing history, the content of the pages you visit, or anything you type into websites.

2. How the information is used

The information is used only to provide the extension's features: saving and restoring each workspace's tabs, syncing your workspaces between your computers, and sharing workspaces with the people you invite. It isn't used for advertising, profiling, analytics, or any purpose unrelated to these features.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Who can see your information

  • You. Everything in your workspaces.
  • Workspace members. When you share a workspace, its members can see its name, resources, notes and tasks, and the email addresses of its members. They can't see your tabs: those stay personal to each member.
  • Service providers. Supabase hosts the database and handles sign-in, and Google authenticates your account. They process the data only to provide those services.

Data is never sold or transferred to anyone else, except where the law requires it.

4. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access rules in the database let each person read only their own workspaces and the workspaces shared with them.

5. Keeping and deleting your information

Your information is kept for as long as you use the extension.

  • Delete a workspace from the dashboard sidebar to remove it, with its tabs, resources, notes and tasks, for you and all its members.
  • Leave a shared workspace from its Share dialog to remove your access and your tabs in it.
  • Remove the extension to delete the copy stored on your computer.
  • Delete your account and all of its data by contacting the developer (see below). This is done within 30 days.

6. Children

The extension isn't directed at children under 13, and doesn't knowingly collect their information.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes are also noted in the extension's Chrome Web Store listing.

8. Contact

For questions about this policy, or to have your account and data deleted, contact the developer at the support email address shown on the Workspace Organizer listing in the Chrome Web Store.
